## 1. Explicit, Document-Specific Consent
Before submitting any identity or business verification document on COVERLY, users must review and explicitly accept a document-specific consent modal recording:
- Document type submitted
- Verification method (`Manual Compliance Review` or explicitly disclosed authorized partner)
- Policy version identifier (`kyc-consent-2026-11-18-v1`)
- UTC acceptance timestamp

## 2. Voluntary Submission & Non-Discrimination for Unregistered Entities
- Small businesses, sole proprietorships, and individual buyers who are not legally required to hold GSTIN, MSME/Udyam, or Certificate of Incorporation are **never forced** to submit inapplicable documents.
- Optional trust documents may be submitted voluntarily to earn individual badges (`GST Verified`, `Udyam Verified`).

## 3. Truthfulness & Authenticity Representation
By consenting and submitting a verification record, the user represents that:
1. The document is genuine, current, and unaltered.
2. The user is legally authorized to submit the document on behalf of the named individual or business entity.
3. Forged, altered, or fraudulent submissions may result in immediate rejection, badge revocation, or account suspension.

## 4. Withdrawal of Consent
Users may withdraw verification consent at any time via COVERLY's privacy request channel. Withdrawal stops ongoing manual review and may result in removal of active verification badges, without affecting the lawfulness of processing performed prior to withdrawal or statutory audit retention.
