## 1. Meaning of Verification Badges
COVERLY displays individual, granular badges (`Mobile Verified`, `Email Verified`, `Identity Verified`, `PAN Verified`, `GST Verified`, `Udyam Verified`, `Business Verified`) based strictly on the specific checks completed and approved for that account or business profile.

## 2. Scope & Limitations of Verification
- Verification confirms that the submitted document or contact channel passed COVERLY's documented format and manual compliance review checks at the time of review.
- Verification badges **do not** constitute a government endorsement, credit rating, solvency guarantee, or commercial warranty of product quality, delivery performance, or payment reliability.
- Buyers and suppliers remain responsible for conducting independent commercial due diligence before entering high-value B2B contracts.

## 3. Rejection, Resubmission & Revocation
- If a submission is incomplete, illegible, expired, or inconsistent with profile details, COVERLY will provide a clear reason and enable structured resubmission in the Verification Center.
- COVERLY reserves the right to mark expired credentials as `Expired`, request re-verification upon material business changes, or suspend/revoke verification badges if misrepresentation or policy violations are detected.

## 4. Security & Anti-Abuse Compliance
Users must not attempt to bypass file validation, upload malicious executables/scripts, abuse OTP endpoints, or submit documents belonging to third parties without written authorization. Violations may trigger automated rate-limiting, IP blocks, and permanent account suspension.
